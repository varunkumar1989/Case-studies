## What the case study covers

- **Flow evaluated:** the owner journey for listing a property, from login and OTP through address, photos and KYC to publish.
- **Findings:** 11 issues and 6 further observations, each mapped to a usability heuristic and a fix.
- **Laws applied:** Jakob's, Hick's, Fitts's and the Peak–End Rule.
- **Output:** prioritised fixes (P1 to P3), success metrics and a usability test plan.
