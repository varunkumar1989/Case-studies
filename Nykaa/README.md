# Nykaa · UX teardown

Five app journeys (login and offers, sunscreen search, face wash order, Nykaa Play, account and wallet), each broken into steps tagged smooth, slow, confusing or frustrating, plus conversations with three users. Independent educational work, not affiliated with Nykaa.

- [`Nykaa_User_Journey_Detailed.pdf`](./Nykaa_User_Journey_Detailed.pdf): 11-page detailed teardown
- [`Nykaa_UX_Analysis_Final_PPT.pdf`](./Nykaa_UX_Analysis_Final_PPT.pdf): 7-page deck
- Claude artifact: [Nykaa UX Teardown](https://claude.ai/artifact/A7z8NgDnJfHCj6vKSRjcvB)

Takeaways: discovery and product trust are strengths (AI-summarised reviews convert); onboarding and checkout leak trust; app-store rating and long-form review sentiment diverge.
