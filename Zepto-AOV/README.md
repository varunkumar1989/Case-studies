# Zepto · increasing AOV

Grow the basket without losing frequency. Zepto's AOV sits near ₹388 against rivals at ₹547 to ₹750+, and the goal is ₹388 → ₹480 in six months. Independent educational case study by Varun Kumar, not affiliated with Zepto.
