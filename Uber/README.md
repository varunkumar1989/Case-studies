# Uber · Find My Ride

Product discovery case study on the last-100-metre pickup problem (Assumptions, Hypotheses, Evidence, Validated insights). Evidence: a 25-response survey and 10 rider interviews in Bengaluru. Independent educational work, not affiliated with Uber. Figures marked "modeled" are research-team estimates, not Uber data.
